# Global Acme Corp Handbook & Policies

## 1. Working Hours & Remote Work
Employees are expected to maintain core collaborative hours between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM local time. Flexible working hours are supported subject to team alignment and manager agreement.

## 2. Annual Leave and Time Off
All full-time staff receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly. Unused leave of up to 5 days may be carried over into the following calendar year, expiring on March 31st.

## 3. Expense Reimbursement
Travel, lodging, and meal expenses incurred during business duties must be submitted via Concur within 30 days of occurrence. Receipts are required for any single expense exceeding $25.

## 4. Information Security & Device Hygiene
Laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled (BitLocker on Windows, FileVault on macOS) and run the latest security patches. Passwords must be at least 16 characters and never reused across systems.
