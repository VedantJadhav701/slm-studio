What it does

Built for people who know the work.

No machine-learning background needed. Pick what you want the model to do, and the app guides the rest.

◎

Start with a goal

Choose Knowledge Q&A, ticket triage, or a command assistant. The app shows the data each path needs and the steps it takes.

⇪

Bring your own data

Drop in JSON, JSONL, CSV, TXT, or Markdown. Every problem row is reported with its file and line number, so you can fix it quickly.

⊘

Duplicates removed, data held back

Repeated records are removed automatically. A share of your data is kept aside to measure the model fairly, and it is never used for training.

▦

Knows your hardware

The app checks your memory and graphics, then recommends models that will run well on your machine, with clear limits where they apply.

⌘

Runs with Ollama Next release

Models will run locally through Ollama on CPU or GPU. Point the app at a server on your own network if your team shares one.

✓

Suggests, never acts alone Planned

The planned command assistant will only suggest. Nothing will run until a person approves it, and every suggestion will be logged.

How it works

Four steps, all on your machine.

  1. 1

    Choose a goal

    Decide what the model should do. The app explains what data that needs.

  2. 2

    Add your data

    Drop files in. The app checks every row and removes duplicates.

  3. 3

    Check your machine

    See which models fit your hardware and whether Ollama is ready.

  4. 4

    Prepare and review

    Review the counts, warnings, and rows that need fixing before anything is trained or used.

Private by design.

  • Your files, prepared datasets, and reports stay on your computer.
  • No telemetry, no accounts, and no cloud training.
  • The app's interface makes no outbound network requests. The only connection it makes is to the Ollama address you set, which you can keep on your own machine or network.
  • Open source, so your security team can read exactly what it does.

Platforms

Windows and macOS.

Windows

Runs on Windows 10 and later. Planned on-device training will use an NVIDIA graphics card.

macOS

Runs on macOS 11 and later. Apple Silicon is recommended for on-device training once it is released.

Features marked “Next release” or “Planned” are in development and are not part of the current version.
